## Style bible

- Minimal thin black-line stick figures with simple expressive faces.
- Stiles: round head, slightly crooked mouth, anxious but readable poses.
- Palette: dusty blue, charcoal, pale beige, restrained warm practical lights.
- No face for the intruder; dirty jeans and worn boots are the recurring identifier.
- No gore, no visible weapon, no invented confrontation.
- Vertical composition with caption-safe negative space.
