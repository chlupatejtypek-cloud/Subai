# Someone Broke In and Searched for Me

## Scene 1 — The closet below

NARRATOR: I was crouched in a pitch-black attic when the closet door opened directly beneath me.
NARRATOR: All I could see was a pair of worn work boots.
NARRATOR: The stranger stood there, searching, while my feet hovered less than a yard above his head.
NARRATOR: And downstairs, one knife was already missing.

## Scene 2 — Three hours earlier

NARRATOR: A Reddit user shared this story from a house he rented in Northern California.
NARRATOR: There were no streetlights, and on cloudy nights the neighborhood disappeared into darkness.
NARRATOR: After an overtime shift, he pulled into the driveway and smelled fresh cigarette smoke.
NARRATOR: He saw no one. Tired, he went inside, showered, and fell asleep before seven.

## Scene 3 — The harmless explanation

NARRATOR: Sometime after nine, a sound woke him.
NARRATOR: His first thought was harmless. A friend had a spare key and occasionally stopped by to shower before night classes.
NARRATOR: But that friend always texted first.
NARRATOR: The phone screen blinded him, and he could not tell whether a message was waiting.

## Scene 4 — The answer

NARRATOR: So he called out his friend’s name.
NARRATOR: For two seconds, the house was silent.
NARRATOR: Then heavy footsteps exploded across the floor below.
NARRATOR: They were not running away. They were charging up the stairs.

## Scene 5 — The attic

NARRATOR: He jumped from bed, reached the closet, and pulled open the ceiling hatch.
NARRATOR: The intruder reached the hallway first—but ran into the bathroom at the far end.
NARRATOR: That single mistake bought just enough time.
NARRATOR: As the bedroom door burst open, the renter dragged his feet into the attic and froze.

## Scene 6 — Searching room by room

NARRATOR: The intruder checked the bedroom, rushed into the spare room, then came back.
NARRATOR: A light snapped on.
NARRATOR: The closet door was ripped open.
NARRATOR: Dirty jeans. Frayed cuffs. Work boots. That was all the renter could see from above.

## Scene 7 — One foot away

NARRATOR: The man stepped back, flipped the bed, and screamed in frustration.
NARRATOR: He was not casually stealing electronics. He was searching places where a person could hide.
NARRATOR: Then he stormed downstairs, overturning furniture and throwing food from the cupboards.
NARRATOR: The renter stayed silent. His cell phone was still beside the bed.

## Scene 8 — Count to one thousand

NARRATOR: When the crashing finally stopped, he did not move.
NARRATOR: He counted slowly to one thousand, climbed down, and called police from the landline.
NARRATOR: The side door had been forced open. Near the fence, police found cigarette butts and objects they associated with drug use.

## Scene 9 — The missing object

NARRATOR: Inside, the sofa was flipped, shelves were emptied, and every room was wrecked.
NARRATOR: But almost nothing had been stolen.
NARRATOR: Almost.
NARRATOR: One knife was missing from the kitchen block.

## Scene 10 — The ritual

NARRATOR: The renter fixed the door, installed motion lights, and even ran a phone line into the attic.
NARRATOR: He lived there three more years without another incident.
NARRATOR: But he kept practicing the same escape—bed, closet, attic—as quickly and quietly as possible.
NARRATOR: If you smelled smoke outside your dark house tonight, would you still walk in?
