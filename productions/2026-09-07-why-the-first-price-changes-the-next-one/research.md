# Research before script — anchoring, 2026-09-08
Primary read: Tversky & Kahneman (1974), Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases, Science 185, 1124–1131; PDF chunk 4, Adjustment and Anchoring.
https://www.cs.tufts.edu/comp/150AIH/pdf/TverskyKa74.pdf
Review corroboration, accessible abstract/introduction:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1053535710001411

Reported wheel anchors 10 vs 65; participants first indicated higher/lower than the quantity, then estimated. Median estimates 25% vs 45%. These are MEDIANS, not means (some popular explainers mislabel them). Not a current estimate of UN membership and not the true answer; chart must explicitly label participant estimates. The arbitrary numerical starting points moved group estimates; no claim everyone is affected equally or that all pricing effects have one mechanism.
Useful angle: an anchor need not be relevant evidence. Translate cautiously to shopping: forming a value/budget and checking real alternatives is a practical suggestion, NOT a guaranteed tested cure. $99/$49 are fictional illustrative price labels, not experimental results or an advertisement.
