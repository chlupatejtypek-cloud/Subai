# Research before script
Roediger & Karpicke2006, Test-Enhanced Learning, Psychological Science17(3)249–255. Original PDF results/methods chunk1 read:
https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2006/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf
Experiment1 undergraduate passage recall: additional study better after5min; retrieval practice better at2days/1week. Experiment2 repeats study/test varied; repeated study inflated confidence despite poorer week-later retention. No data chart in this video, no universal guarantee. Immediate vs delayed boundary retained in narration.
Corroboration read (discussion of the same paper, not independent replication):
https://blog.ctl.gatech.edu/2017/11/15/testing-is-a-key-to-better-learning/
Practical advice to self-quiz, check answers and revisit later. Original2006 experiments used free recall without feedback; our feedback/correct-notes step is practical educational advice, NOT represented as that study's precise intervention. Learners should not rehearse uncorrected mistakes. No claim rereading is useless.
