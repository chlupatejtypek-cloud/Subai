# Why Everyone Seems to Notice Your Mistakes

One awkward moment can feel like a roomful of attention. That feeling isn't a head count.

Stiles illustrates the spotlight effect: our own vivid experience can make us overestimate how much others notice our appearance and behavior. Try separating what you actually observed from what you are guessing, then return to the conversation.

Research: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10707330/
Accessible explanation: https://thedecisionlab.com/biases/spotlight-effect

General psychology education, not diagnosis or individualized mental-health advice. The cafe and duck-shirt scenes are AI-generated illustrations, not footage of the original experiment.
