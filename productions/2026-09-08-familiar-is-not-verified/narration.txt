Hearing a claim again isn't the same as getting new evidence. But it can feel that way. Repetition makes a statement easier to process, and that familiar feeling can get mistaken for truth. Researchers call this the illusory truth effect. The unsettling part? It can happen even when people already know facts that contradict the claim. Knowing better doesn't always mean using that knowledge. So before sharing something that sounds familiar, ask a different question: where did the evidence come from? Three posts repeating one source are not three independent confirmations. Trace the claim back to the original evidence, and check whether another reliable source actually supports it. This isn't a guarantee against mistakes. It's a reminder: familiar is a feeling. Verified takes a check.
