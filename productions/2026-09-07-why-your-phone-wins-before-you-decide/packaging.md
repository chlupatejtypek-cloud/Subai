# Why Your Phone Wins Before You Decide

Ever unlock your phone and wonder what you meant to check?

Stiles explores the checking habit: brief, repeated looks at a phone, sometimes triggered by a quiet moment. New information can reinforce another check. For your next work block, try putting the phone out of sight and out of reach. This is a small experiment, not a cure or a guarantee.

Research:
Oulasvirta et al. (2012), Habits make smartphone use more pervasive.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-011-0412-2
University of Washington, Patterns of compulsive smartphone use suggest how to kick the habit (2019).
https://www.washington.edu/news/2019/04/29/patterns-of-compulsive-smartphone-use/

General psychology education, not diagnosis or personalized medical advice. Scenes and narration are AI-generated illustrations. No music.
