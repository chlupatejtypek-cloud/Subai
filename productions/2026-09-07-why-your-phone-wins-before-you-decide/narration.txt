You unlocked your phone. But what were you going to check?
Sometimes, there wasn't a plan.
Researchers call brief, repeated looks at your phone a checking habit.
A quiet moment can become the cue.
A quick look brings new information, and that can reinforce another check.
So this isn't always a willpower contest.
Try changing the setup: for your next work block, put the phone out of sight and out of reach.
Not a cure. Just one less easy invitation.
Give the next check a reason, instead of letting the pause decide.
