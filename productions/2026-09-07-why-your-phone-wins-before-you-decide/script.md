# Why Your Phone Wins Before You Decide

NARRATOR: You unlocked your phone. But what were you going to check?

NARRATOR: Sometimes, there wasn't a plan.

NARRATOR: Researchers call brief, repeated looks at your phone a checking habit.

NARRATOR: A quiet moment can become the cue.

NARRATOR: A quick look brings new information, and that can reinforce another check.

NARRATOR: So this isn't always a willpower contest.

NARRATOR: Try changing the setup: for your next work block, put the phone out of sight and out of reach.

NARRATOR: Not a cure. Just one less easy invitation.

NARRATOR: Give the next check a reason, instead of letting the pause decide.
