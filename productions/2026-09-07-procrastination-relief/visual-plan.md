# Visual plan — Why Procrastination Feels Like Relief

All scenes are 9:16 premium minimalist Stiles stickman art. No baked-in text. Keep the lower-center caption area clean.

1. **Hook / Agnes, ~0–4 s:** Stiles frozen at a desk between an open laptop and phone. A dark blue discomfort cloud descends while the phone emits an inviting amber light sweep. Fixed camera; only cloud and light move.
2. **Difficult assignment:** Stiles faces a blank laptop/document; three abstract shapes suggest uncertainty, boredom, and fear without written labels. Slow push-in.
3. **Phone relief:** Stiles reaches toward the glowing phone while the dark cloud temporarily retreats. Gentle horizontal pan toward phone.
4. **Learned loop:** circular path visually links task discomfort, phone, brief relief, and returning stress. Slow pull-out.
5. **Future-self cost:** present Stiles passes an oversized stack of papers toward a worried future Stiles. Slow horizontal pan.
6. **Name the feeling:** Stiles calmly observes a small cloud rather than fleeing; clean breathing room. Slow push-in.
7. **If–then action:** after-dinner desk cue; Stiles opens a file and writes one tiny first line. Slow push-in or lateral pan according to generated composition.
8. **Payoff:** tiny doorway-sized first step leading into a brighter path; Stiles steps through confidently. Slow pull-out.
