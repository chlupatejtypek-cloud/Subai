# Packaging

## Title

**Why Procrastination Feels Like Relief**

## Description

Procrastination can briefly reduce the discomfort attached to a task—teaching avoidance to repeat. This Short explains the psychology of short-term mood repair and one small if–then plan that can make starting easier.

Research:
- Sirois & Pychyl (2013): https://doi.org/10.1111/spc3.12011
- Open manuscript: https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/91793/
- Implementation-intention overview: https://www.psychologytoday.com/gb/blog/dont-delay/201001/implementation-intentions-facilitate-action-control

General psychology education only; not diagnosis or individualized treatment.
