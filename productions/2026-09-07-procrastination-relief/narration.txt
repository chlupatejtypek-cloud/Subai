Your brain may be rewarding procrastination—and the reward is relief. Open a difficult assignment: uncertainty, boredom, maybe fear appears. So you check your phone. The discomfort drops instantly. Your brain learns, avoiding this task makes me feel better. Psychologists call this short-term mood repair. Today's relief becomes tomorrow's stress. Try this: name the feeling, then make one if-then plan. If I sit down after dinner, then I open the file and write one sentence. Don't wait to feel motivated. Make starting too small to fear.
