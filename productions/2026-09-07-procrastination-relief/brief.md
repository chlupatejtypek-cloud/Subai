# Brief

- Working/final title: **Why Procrastination Feels Like Relief**
- Pillar: Everyday Mind
- Format: 9:16 psychology Short, ≤40 seconds
- Audience tension: “I know what I need to do, so why do I avoid it?”
- Mechanism: aversive feeling → avoidance → immediate mood relief → learned repetition → future-self cost
- Action: name the feeling and create one tiny cue-linked if–then start
- Boundary: educational, not diagnostic; do not claim one mechanism explains every instance of procrastination
